# Postmortem Process

Last updated: February 23, 2026

## When required
- Mandatory for SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents.
- Recommended for recurring SEV-3 incidents.

## Timeline
- Draft within 24 hours of incident resolution.
- Finalized within 5 business days.

## Required sections
1. Incident summary (what happened)
2. Impact (who/what was affected)
3. Timeline (UTC, minute-level for SEV-1/2)
4. Root cause (technical + process)
5. Detection and alert performance
6. What worked / what failed
7. Corrective actions with owners and due dates
8. Policy/process updates required

## Ownership
- Incident commander owns initial draft.
- Platform owner approves final postmortem.

## Success metric
No postmortem is considered complete until all corrective actions are tracked in backlog with due dates.
